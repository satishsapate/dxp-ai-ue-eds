Pricing FAQ

Common Questions

Is there a free trial available?

+
Yes. The Starter and Professional plans both offer a 30-day free trial with full platform access and no credit card required. Our team will guide you through onboarding during the trial period.

Can I change plans as my needs grow?

+
Absolutely. You can upgrade at any time and only pay the prorated difference. Downgrading is available at the end of your billing cycle. Our team will help you manage transitions smoothly.

What counts as a "website" in the Starter plan?

+
A website is defined as a unique domain or subdomain managed through the DXP AI platform. Microsites, landing page collections, and portals each count as one. The Professional and Enterprise plans have no website limits.

Are ZensAI AI features included in all plans?

+
Core AI features (semantic search, content tagging, basic recommendations) are included in all plans. Advanced ZensAI capabilities (conversational AI, content generation, predictive analytics) are available in Professional and above.
