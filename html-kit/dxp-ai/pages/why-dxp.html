AI-First DXP

Traditional DXPs were built for the web era. DXP AI is built for the intelligence era — where every experience is personalized, every decision is data-driven, and AI is woven into every interaction.

DXP vs CMS

What Makes a DXP Different From a CMS?

A CMS manages content. A DXP manages experiences. This fundamental difference shapes how modern organizations think about their digital infrastructure.

A Content Management System (CMS) is focused on a single job: helping authors create, edit, and publish web content. It answers the question: How do we put content on our website?

A Digital Experience Platform (DXP) answers a far bigger question: How do we deliver the right experience to every person, on every channel, at every moment — and continuously improve it?

The 5 Key Differences

  1. Scope — A CMS manages one website. A DXP manages the entire digital customer journey across every touchpoint.
  2. Intelligence — A CMS displays content. A DXP personalizes, predicts, and adapts content based on user context and behavior.
  3. Integration — A CMS sits in isolation. A DXP connects to CRM, ERP, marketing automation, and analytics as a central hub.
  4. Analytics — A CMS has basic page views. A DXP delivers deep behavioral analytics, journey mapping, and conversion optimization.
  5. AI Capability — A CMS has no native AI. DXP AI has ZensAI intelligence embedded throughout — from search to content ops to personalization.
💡
Bottom line: If you're managing a single brochure website, a CMS may be enough. If you're managing digital experiences across multiple brands, channels, and customer segments — you need a DXP.
