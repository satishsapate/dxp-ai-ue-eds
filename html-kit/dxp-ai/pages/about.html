DXP AI is a flagship product of ZensAI, the AI innovation arm of Zensar Technologies — a global digital solutions company with over 30 years of enterprise technology leadership.

Our Story

From Enterprise IT to AI-First Experiences

"We didn't set out to build another CMS. We set out to solve the most important challenge facing every organization: how to deliver experiences that actually matter to people, at scale."

Zensar Technologies has been at the forefront of enterprise digital transformation since 1991. Over three decades, we've worked with Fortune 500 companies, global banks, healthcare systems, and government bodies — helping them navigate each wave of digital change.

When AI began reshaping every industry, we recognized a gap: existing digital experience platforms weren't built for the intelligence era. They were built for the web era — static, expensive to deploy, and bolted-on AI at best.

The ZensAI Vision

In 2022, Zensar established ZensAI as a dedicated AI innovation lab with a single mission: build the world's first truly AI-native Digital Experience Platform. Not a CMS with AI features sprinkled on top — but a platform where intelligence is the foundation, not an afterthought.

DXP AI launched in 2024, and in its first year it has been adopted by 500+ enterprise organizations across banking, retail, healthcare, manufacturing, and government sectors globally.

About Zensar Technologies

Zensar Technologies Ltd. (NSE: ZENSARTECH) is a leading digital solutions and technology services company, part of the RPG Group. Headquartered in Pune, India, Zensar has 10,000+ professionals across 30+ global offices and serves 200+ clients in 30+ countries.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

1991

Zensar Technologies Founded

Zensar Technologies established in Pune, India as part of the RPG Group — beginning a 30-year journey in enterprise technology services.

2022

ZensAI Lab Established

Zensar creates ZensAI as a dedicated AI innovation unit, with a mission to build AI-native enterprise products for the intelligence era.

2023

DXP AI Beta Launch

DXP AI enters private beta with 20 design partners. Core platform including AI Content Management, Personalization Engine, and ZensAI Search shipped.

2024

General Availability & 500+ Clients

DXP AI goes GA. Achieves 500+ enterprise client milestone within the first year. Conversational AI and Predictive Analytics shipped.

2025

ZensAI v2 & Global Expansion

ZensAI v2 launches with next-generation content generation, multi-modal search, and agentic AI capabilities. Expanding to 50+ countries.
