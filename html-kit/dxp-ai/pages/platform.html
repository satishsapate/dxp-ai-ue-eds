Core Architecture

Built for the AI Era

Every layer of DXP AI is engineered for intelligence, composability, and enterprise scale — from the API layer to the end-user experience.

📄
Content Layer

AI Content Management

Create, manage, and reuse structured and unstructured content across every channel with AI-assisted authoring, smart tagging, and workflow automation.

  • WYSIWYG and structured content editors
  • AI-assisted copywriting & summarization
  • Role-based authoring & multi-step approval
  • Digital Asset Management (DAM)
  • Headless content via GraphQL & REST APIs
🎯
Experience Layer

Personalization Engine

Serve hyper-relevant content to every visitor in real time using ML-driven segmentation, behavioral targeting, and intelligent recommendations.

  • Real-time behavioral profiling
  • Rule-based & ML-driven segments
  • A/B, multivariate & bandit testing
  • Contextual recommendations engine
  • Progressive user profile enrichment
📡
Delivery Layer

Multi-Channel Delivery

Deliver consistent, high-performance experiences to websites, mobile apps, portals, voice interfaces, and headless frontends — all from one source of truth.

  • Web, mobile & PWA delivery
  • Customer, employee & partner portals
  • Headless & composable APIs
  • Edge CDN with global distribution
  • Email, push & in-app notification delivery
🤖
AI Layer

ZensAI Intelligence

The AI brain of the platform — powering intelligent search, conversational interfaces, predictive analytics, and automated content operations.

  • Semantic AI-driven search
  • Conversational AI & virtual agents
  • Content generation & summarization
  • Predictive lead & content scoring
  • Automated tagging & classification
📊
Analytics Layer

Analytics & Insights

Understand every interaction with real-time dashboards, funnel analysis, and AI-powered insights that help you continuously optimize experiences.

  • Real-time engagement dashboards
  • Content performance & conversion tracking
  • Audience segmentation reports
  • Custom KPI dashboards
  • Data export & BI integrations
🔗
Integration Layer

Integration Hub

Connect DXP AI to your entire enterprise ecosystem — CRM, ERP, marketing automation, analytics tools, and more — via open APIs and pre-built connectors.

  • Salesforce, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics
  • Marketo, HubSpot, Pardot
  • 50+ pre-built enterprise connectors
  • RESTful APIs & webhooks
  • Custom connector SDK
