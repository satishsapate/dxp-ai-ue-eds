Security FAQ

Common Security Questions

How does DXP AI handle customer data?

+
All customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Data is isolated per-tenant, never shared, and stored in the region you select at onboarding. We never sell or share customer data with third parties.

Can we get our own encryption keys (CMEK)?

+
Yes. Enterprise plan customers can bring their own encryption keys (BYOK/CMEK) managed via AWS KMS, Azure Key Vault, or Google Cloud KMS. Your keys, your control.

What is the SLA for security incident response?

+
Critical security incidents receive a response within 1 hour, initial triage within 4 hours, and full remediation within 24 hours. All customers are notified within 72 hours of any confirmed data breach, as required by GDPR.

Is DXP AI compliant with India's DPDP Act?

+
Yes. DXP AI has implemented the necessary controls to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, including data fiduciary obligations, consent management, and data localization options for Indian customers.
