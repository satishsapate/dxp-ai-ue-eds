What is a Digital Experience Platform?

"A DXP is a platform that enables organizations to deliver personalized, consistent, and engaging digital experiences across all customer touchpoints."

In this article
  1. The Definition
  2. What a DXP Does
  3. Who Uses a DXP
  4. AI-Powered DXPs

A Digital Experience Platform (DXP) is a unified technology platform used by organizations to create, manage, deliver, and optimize digital experiences across multiple channels — such as websites, mobile apps, portals, chatbots, and email.

Think of it as the central hub that connects content, data, personalization, analytics, and integrations to deliver a consistent and engaging user experience at every touchpoint.

What Does a DXP Do?

A DXP helps organizations across seven core capability areas:

  1. Content Management — Create and manage digital content and reuse it across channels with role-based authoring and approvals.
  2. Personalized Experiences — Deliver personalized content based on user behavior, profile, or context.
  3. Multi-Channel Delivery — Serve content across websites, mobile apps, portals, and headless APIs.
  4. Customer & User Engagement — Enable self-service portals, improve customer journeys, integrate chatbots and AI assistants.
  5. Integration Hub — Connect with Salesforce CRM, SAP ERP, and marketing automation platforms.
  6. Analytics & Insights — Track user behavior, measure content performance, and optimize based on data.
  7. AI-Powered Capabilities — AI-driven search, content generation, intelligent recommendations, and conversational AI.
ZensAI DXP Advantage: Unlike traditional DXPs, DXP AI has ZensAI intelligence embedded at the core — not bolted on — meaning every capability is supercharged with AI from day one.

Who Uses a DXP?

  • Marketing teams — content, campaigns, personalization
  • Business teams — customer engagement, portals, self-service
  • IT teams — platform management, integrations, scalability
